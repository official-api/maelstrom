SOLID PROPELLANT ROCKET ENGINE

MAELSTROM's aft 300mm houses a composite solid-propellant grain — a HTPB/AP/Al formulation delivering high specific impulse with zero pre-launch preparation. The propellant burns from the core outward in a regressive grain pattern, generating a consistent high-thrust impulse that accelerates the round to intercept velocity in under 2 seconds. Nozzle throat and expansion ratio are optimised for sea-level performance.

ACTIVE CONTROL FIN AEROFOIL

Four rectangular all-moving control fins at the aft section provide 3-axis manoeuvring authority. The NACA 0008 symmetric profile minimises drag at supersonic transitions while maintaining sufficient lift for rapid course corrections. Actuated by miniaturised electromechanical servos, they respond within 15ms — enabling the tight pursuit curves needed for a closing, evasive drone cluster.

OPTICAL TRACKING & GUIDANCE

An onboard LWIR sensor feeds a lightweight convolutional neural network running at 120fps on an edge TPU. Each drone is detected and assigned a bounding box; the centroid of the cluster bounding box becomes the aim point. Proportional navigation guidance drives the control fins to null the line-of-sight rate, ensuring impact at the geometric centre of the swarm for maximum coverage.

WARHEAD DEPLOYMENT

High-density tungsten fragmentation sleeve surrounds the warhead core. On fuze trigger, a precisely timed explosive ring shears the sleeve into hundreds of high-velocity fragments with a controlled dispersion cone matched to the swarm spread. Fragments achieve > 400 m/s lateral velocity, defeating drone airframes and rotor assemblies within a 15m radius sphere.
